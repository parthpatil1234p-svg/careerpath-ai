10. CONTINGENCY FALLBACK PLANS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Scenario 1: Local Backend Server Not Running
- Cause: Node process stopped or port conflict.
- Fix: Immediately switch `client/js/config.js` or navigate directly to the live production deployment: `https://careerpath-ai-jade.vercel.app`. The Vercel deployment points to Render and runs 24/7.

Scenario 2: Slow Initial Backend Response on Render
- Cause: Free-tier Render instances sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity (cold start ~30s).
- Fix: Warm up the backend 2 minutes before recording by visiting `https://careerpath-ai-bdbt.onrender.com/api/health`.

Scenario 3: WebGL Hardware Acceleration Disabled or Glitching
