How Career Atlas Works

A transparent 3-step navigation process from self-assessment to practical task completion.

01 · PROFILE

Understand your profile

Share your degree, fields that interest you, and the technical skills you already possess along with your current self-assessed proficiency.

02 · EVALUATION

Compare skills with career paths

Our deterministic weighted model compares your capabilities against 5 industry careers: 60% skill alignment, 25% interest fit, and 15% educational relevance.

03 · EXECUTION

Follow your learning route

Receive a customized 4, 8, or 12-week roadmap that targets your missing skills first with curated documentation, practice tasks, and portfolio projects.
